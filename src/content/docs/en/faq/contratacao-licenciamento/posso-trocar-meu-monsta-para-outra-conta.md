---
title: Can I switch my Monsta to another account?
---
Yes, you can switch the account used by your Monsta; however, alert credits and users registered on Telegram are not migrated and will remain on the old account.

To change your account in Monsta, follow these steps:

- Click the gear in the upper right corner;
- Select "About Monsta";
- Select the "License and account" tab;
- Click the "Switch account" button.

The login screen will appear and you can enter the new account credentials. After logging in, a screen with the licenses available for use will be displayed. Click the "Select" button for the key you wish to use. After that, your Monsta will be connected to your new account on our website.

:::note[Nota]  
If you wish to change the email address of your account in the Client Area, see the article "[How to change the email address of my account in the client area?](https://docs.monsta.com.br/pt-br/faq/contratacao-licenciamento/como-alterar-o-endereco-de-e-mail-da-minha-conta-na-area-do-cliente/)".  
:::
