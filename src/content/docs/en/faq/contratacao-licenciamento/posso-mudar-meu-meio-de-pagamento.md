---
title: Can I change my payment method?
description: Change payment method
---
"Yes, you can change your payment method at any time in the Client Area by accessing the option **'Subscriptions / Payment Manager'**."

> **Note:** In Brazil, if a boleto has already been issued for the current invoice and you change the payment method to credit card, you will need to wait for that boleto to reach its due date and expire. As soon as the boleto expires, the charge will be automatically made to the registered credit card, provided it is set as the **default payment method**. It is not possible to manually prepay the open invoice on the card while the boleto is pending.
