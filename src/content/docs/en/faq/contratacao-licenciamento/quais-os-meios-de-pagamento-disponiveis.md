title: What payment methods are available?
description: Payment methods
---
**Payment methods in Brazil:**

In Brazil, we accept **Credit Card** and **Boleto (bank slip)**. The card offers instant approval and automatic renewal, while the boleto has a due date of up to 3 days, a bank clearing time of 1 to 3 business days and automatic renewal.

**Payment methods in other countries:**

For customers outside Brazil, we accept exclusively **Credit Card.** Approval is immediate and renewal is automatic.

**How does subscription activation work after payment?**

- **Credit Card:** The activation or renewal of the service occurs immediately.
- **Boleto (bank slip):** Access is granted automatically once the payment clearing is confirmed by the bank (usually on the next business day).
