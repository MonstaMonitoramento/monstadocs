description: See how to customize the email notifications for your Monsta account and choose which content, news, and new-release alerts you want to receive.
---
The [Monsta Customer Area](https://store.monsta.com.br/) allows you to manage and customize the communications and notifications you want to receive by email about your account. Follow the steps below to adjust your preferences:

## Step-by-step to change your email notifications

### 1. Access Account Preferences

On the [Customer Area](https://store.monsta.com.br/account/preferences), click the profile icon located in the **top right corner** of the screen and select **Preferences** from the drop-down menu.

![example1.png](/src/assets/images/mvjub2pe-cl9a999n.png)  

### 2. Change the content options for email notifications

On the preferences page, scroll down to the **Email notifications** section (located just below the access email change block).

![example5.png](/src/assets/images/mvjv1sim-rft4fpfy.png)

Use the toggle switches next to each option to enable or disable the types of messages you want to receive:

- **Monsta tips and best practices:** Guidance and recommendations to make the most of the features in day-to-day use.
- **News:** General news and updates about Monsta.
- **New release:** Alerts and notices about system update releases.

### 3. Save the changes

After adjusting the switches according to your preference, click the **Save preferences** button.

Done! Your preferences have been saved and you will start receiving only the selected communications.
