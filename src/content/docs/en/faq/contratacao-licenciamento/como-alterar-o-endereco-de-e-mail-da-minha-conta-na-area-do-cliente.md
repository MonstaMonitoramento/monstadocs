title: How do I change my account email address in the Client Area?
description: Learn how to change the email address of your Monsta Client Area account in a few simple steps and keep your login details up to date.
---
To change the email address registered on your account in the [Client Area](https://store.monsta.com.br/) of Monsta, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

## Step-by-step instructions to change

### 1. Access Account Preferences

After logging in to the [Client Area](https://store.monsta.com.br/account/preferences), click the user options icon located in the **top right corner** of the screen and select **Preferences**.
  
![exemplo1.png](/src/assets/images/mvjub2pe-cl9a999n.png)  

### 2. Enter the new email address
  
On the **Preferences** screen, in the **New email** field, type the new address you want to use to access your account and click the **Send confirmation** button.

![exemplo2.png](/src/assets/images/mvjuc4s0-h5vn6y1b.png)

### 3. Check your inbox

You will receive a message sent from `no-reply@monsta.com.br`. Open the email and click the **Confirm new email** button.

![exemplo3.png](/src/assets/images/mvjucesi-mcllicf6.png)

:::note[Note]
The confirmation link is valid for **1 hour**.
:::

### 4. Confirm the change

When you click the link, a new browser page will open. Click the **Confirm new email** button to finish the process.

![exemplo4.png](/src/assets/images/mvjucs3a-u4gsdmr4.png)

Done! Your email has been successfully changed. The current session will be ended and you will be redirected to the login page, where you can access the client area using the **new email address** and the **same password**.
