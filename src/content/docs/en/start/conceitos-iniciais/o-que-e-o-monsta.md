Monsta, developed by Monsta Tecnologia, is a **monitoring platform for Information Technology (IT) infrastructure** designed to provide a comprehensive, real-time view of the health and performance of various components within an IT environment. It enables companies and IT professionals to identify, diagnose, and resolve infrastructure issues proactively, ensuring service continuity and resource optimization.

**Main Features and Functionalities**:

- **Intuitive and Dynamic Graphic Dashboards**: The platform offers customizable visualization dashboards that present crucial metrics and information in a clear and easy-to-understand way. These dashboards can be tailored to display specific data relevant to different areas of the infrastructure.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Monsta collects and displays performance data in real time, allowing users to follow the activity and status of their systems at the exact moment events occur.
- **Accurate Problem Detection**: Through threshold configurations and intelligent alerts, Monsta can identify anomalies and potential performance issues before they cause significant outages.
- **Alerts and Notifications**: The alerting system notifies users via channels such as SMS, email, and Telegram about critical events or issues requiring attention, enabling a fast and efficient response.
- **Automatic Cloud Backup**: Monsta offers automatic backup of configurations and monitoring data to the cloud, ensuring the security and availability of this information in case of local failures.
- **Monitoring of Devices and Services**: The platform is scalable and capable of monitoring a wide range of infrastructure devices (servers, networks, firewalls, etc.) and services (applications, databases, websites, etc.), with the possibility of monitoring an unlimited number of items depending on the subscribed plan.
- **Remote Network Monitoring via Agents**: Monsta allows extending infrastructure visibility beyond the local network, reaching remote sites, external clients, or cloud environments securely and simply through its agents.
