Monsta is a flexible and robust monitoring platform designed to adapt to different scenarios and business models. If your operation depends on a stable, secure, and high-performance Information Technology (IT) infrastructure, Monsta was built for you.

See how our solution specifically serves the main market profiles:

## 1. Internet Service Providers (ISPs)

For providers, stability and latency are everything. Monsta is the right-hand of your NOC, allowing you to proactively monitor the health of the entire network and act before the customer notices the outage.

- **End-to-End Visibility:** Monitor from backbone equipment, edge routers and OLTs to Points of Presence (PoPs).
- **Multi-vendor:** Native SNMP integration to collect crucial metrics on traffic, signal, and CPU from leading market brands (such as Huawei, Cisco, Mikrotik, Intelbras, Fiberhome and Datacom).
- **Faster Response:** Immediate alerts drastically reduce MTTR (_Mean Time to Resolution_ — Average Time to Resolution).

## 2. Managed Service Providers (MSPs)

Managing the IT of dozens of clients without centralized tools is a scalable challenge. Monsta enables MSPs to raise their delivery level with efficient multi-tenant monitoring.

- **Centralized Operation:** Track the infrastructure of multiple clients on a single screen, ensuring compliance with strict SLAs.
- **Lightweight Agents:** Collect data from remote networks with efficient agents that do not overload the client environment.
- **Competitive Edge:** Turn reactive support into a consultative and preventive service, generating capacity and availability reports for your clients.

## 3. IT and Network Consultants

As an independent consultant or project-focused professional, you need a tool that is quick to deploy, reliable, and validates the value of your work.

- **Easy Deployment:** No steep learning curves or endless configurations. You install, configure discoveries, and start collecting data quickly.
- **Root Cause Diagnosis:** Identify hidden bottlenecks in a client's network (interface errors, link saturation, hardware failures) using metric histories and detailed graphs.
- **Tailored Monitoring:** Create dashboards and custom alerts according to the specific needs of each project.
- **Monthly Reports via AI:** Artificial Intelligence that transforms asset and alert data into strategic insights, emailed at the start of each month.

## 4. Companies in General (SMEs to Large Corporations)

Whether to ensure an e-commerce's sales, keep an industrial system running, or ensure that healthcare and finance services don't stop, Monsta protects your business productivity.

- **Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs):** Have the monitoring power of a large corporation with an intuitive interface, ideal for lean teams that need to focus on the business, not on maintaining the tool.
- **Large Enterprises and ITOps:** Support for complex network topologies, monitoring of critical servers, databases and applications, with centralized views and dashboards for quick decision-making.
