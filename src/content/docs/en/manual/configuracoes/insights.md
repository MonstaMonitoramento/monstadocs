The **Insights** feature automatically generates a monthly report with an intelligent analysis of the monitored environment. At the beginning of each month, Monsta consolidates data from the previous month (when available) and uses artificial intelligence to identify patterns, trends and areas of concern, delivering a clear and objective summary of the infrastructure's behavior.

The report is sent to the email address configured by the user and is intended to make it easier to interpret the information collected by the system, turning large volumes of data into observations and recommendations useful for managing the environment.

To produce the analysis, the artificial intelligence uses information such as:

- Total alerts sent, grouped by type;
- Total events recorded, organized by device and monitor;
- The five devices and monitors with the highest number of events in the period;
- Number of assets and monitors configured in the environment;
- Comparison with the previous month, when data is available.

Based on these data, the report can highlight trends, identify devices or monitors with recurring behavior, point out significant changes compared to the previous period, and present recommendations that help prioritize preventive and corrective actions.

The analyses presented are automatically generated by artificial intelligence from the data collected by Monsta and should be used to support decision-making. Although they provide relevant information about the monitored environment, they do not replace the technical analysis performed by the administrator.
